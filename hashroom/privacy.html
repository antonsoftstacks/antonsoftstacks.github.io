Hash Room - Privacy Policy

AntonSoft Studios · Last updated 13 November 2026

In short: Hash Room has no ads and no analytics, and doesn't ask for your name, email or location. Your game is saved on your phone. If you choose to, it's also backed up to your own Google Play account. The online leaderboard only knows the nickname you pick, your score, and a random ID the game makes up.

Who we are

Hash Room is made by Anton Sigheartau, trading as AntonSoft Studios, in the United Kingdom. We are responsible for the personal data described here. You can contact us at antonsigheartauaz@gmail.com.

What the game stores on your phone

Your progress (rigs, coins, money, settings and so on) is saved in the game's own storage on your device. It stays there and is deleted if you uninstall the game. Photos you take with the in-game Photo button are saved to your phone's Pictures folder, and are only shared if you share them yourself.

What leaves your phone, and why

ServiceWhat is sentWhy
CheddaBoards (online leaderboard) A random player ID created by the game, the leaderboard nickname you choose, and your total coins mined. Your phone's internet address is seen by the server, as with any website. To show the worldwide leaderboard. Your nickname and score are visible to other players.
Google Play Games Services If you sign in: your Play Games profile and a copy of your saved game. To sign you in and keep a cloud backup of your progress, so you can carry on after changing or resetting your phone. The backup is stored in your own Google account.
Google Play (store, in-app updates) Information Google Play already handles for any app, such as your purchase and installed version. To sell the game, check your copy is genuine, and offer updates inside the game.

We don't sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it to track you across other apps.

Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. CheddaBoards is a leaderboard service run by Chedda Tech; see cheddaboards.com.

Notifications

If you allow them, the game schedules reminders (for example when a rig overheats or a bill is due) on your phone itself. No notification data is sent to us.

Legal basis (UK GDPR)

We use the leaderboard and cloud-save data to provide features you choose to use (performance of a contract, and our legitimate interest in running a fair leaderboard). Signing in to Google Play Games is optional.

How long it's kept

  • On your phone: until you reset your progress in Settings or uninstall the game.
  • Leaderboard entries: while the leaderboard runs, or until you ask us to remove yours.
  • Cloud saves: in your Google account until you delete them. You can do this from Google Play Games or your Google account's data settings.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you, or object to its use. Email us with your leaderboard nickname and we'll deal with it within one month. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

Hash Room isn't aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. Leaderboard nicknames should not include real names.

About the coins in the game

Hershcoin and every other coin, exchange and company in Hash Room are made up. Nothing in the game can be exchanged for real money or cryptocurrency.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
